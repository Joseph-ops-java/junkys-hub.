What is Junky's Hub?

Junky's Hub is my digital brand where creativity, street influence, and tech energy collide — a space for expression, motion, and design freedom.

Expect weekly drops, behind-the-scenes experiments, and a growing collection of visuals you can use across devices. The Hub is about momentum — small releases that build a bigger vibe.

Brand Notes

  • Palette: Terracotta Orange, Teal, Purple on deep black.
  • Type: Inter (UI) + Bebas Neue (display / headings).
  • Aesthetic: Bold, minimal, comic‑inspired gradients with motion.
